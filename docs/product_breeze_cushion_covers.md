## Usage and Care
- Machine wash cold on a gentle cycle, inside out, with like colours.
- Line dry in shade; tumble drying can shrink cotton and fade the wash.
- Iron on medium with the zip closed.
- Expect a soft, lived-in fade over time — copy may call this "sun-washed character", never a flaw.
