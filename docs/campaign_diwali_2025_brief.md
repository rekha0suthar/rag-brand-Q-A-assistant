# Diwali 2025 Campaign Brief

## Overview
- Theme: "Ghar ki roshni" — the light a home already has; our products just let it out.
- Flight: 1 October to 2 November 2025.
- Channels: Instagram, YouTube Shorts, email, plus a festive landing page on the website.
- Hero products: Glow Diya Set first, Hearth Candle Trio second, festive bundles from the Celebration band.

## Offer
- Flat 10% off on orders above ₹999. One discount, one threshold — exactly as the pricing policy and the Summer 2025 retro demand.
- We are deliberately NOT repeating the Diwali 2024 "Buy 2 Get 10% Off" structure: it performed, but quantity offers confused shoppers when any other message was in market. Flat-above-threshold is simpler to say in one line.
- Mandatory disclaimer on every offer asset: "Limited-time offer; terms apply."
- The 10% sits comfortably under the 20% discount ceiling; no exception paperwork needed.

## What We Learned From Diwali 2024
- "Light up" and "gifting" language resonated; keep both families of words in rotation.
- Short headlines with sparing emoji worked; that is now standard practice brand-wide.
- The reference line "Light up your gifting game ✨" remains approved. Reuse it as a callback, not as the lead line — 2025 needs its own voice.

## Creative Direction
- Rituals over abstractions: lighting diyas, cleaning the house, gifting sweets, the first rangoli attempt that goes slightly wrong. Specific beats generic "celebrate the season" every time (tone FAQ rule).
- Gifting is the emotional centre: every hero asset shows a product being given, not just displayed.
- The handwritten-style gift note at checkout gets its own story frame — it is the most Diwali-shaped feature we have.
- Palette: deep festive tones, flame light, marigold. One emoji max per caption; none in email subjects.

## Copy Starters (directions, not yet approved lines)
- "Ghar ki roshni, thodi aur." (Hinglish welcome on social.)
- "Six little lights, one warm welcome." (Glow Diya Set reference line — already approved on the spec sheet.)
- "The gift that's already wrapped."
- Email subject direction: benefit-led, no emoji, e.g. "Diwali gifting, sorted by Sunday."

## What To Avoid
- No superlatives, ever — and watch for festive creep like "the brightest Diwali yet", which is a superlative in costume.
- No "deal of the season" or similar; it was flagged in Summer 2025 review and stays banned.
- No urgency theatrics: no countdowns without a real end date, no "only 2 left" without inventory data.
- No mixing the offer with the free-shipping threshold in one line; keep "Flat 10% off above ₹999" and "free shipping above ₹1,499" in separate sentences if both must appear.

## Timeline and Process
- Asset lock: 15 September. Compliance review booked for the week of 15 September — two business days turnaround, and festive season is the queue's worst traffic; late assets will miss the first drop.
- Approvals from Diwali 2024 have expired (approvals last 90 days); every reused 2024 asset goes back through review.
- Landing page copy counts as marketing copy and goes through the same review.

## Success Measures (directional)
- Gifting-keyword engagement against Diwali 2024.
- Share of orders using the gift note at checkout during the flight.
- Bundle attach rate on the Glow Diya Set.
