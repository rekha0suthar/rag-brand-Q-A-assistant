# Returns, Replacements and Customer Support Policy

The customer-facing rules our support team applies, and the approved language for talking about them. Marketing copy that mentions returns must match this document exactly — an over-promised return window is a compliance problem.

## Return Window
- 10 days from delivery for a change-of-mind return, on unused products in their original packaging.
- The window is counted from the delivery date in the courier's record, not the order date.
- Returns are free: we arrange a reverse pickup at no charge. If reverse pickup is unavailable at the customer's pincode, support arranges an alternative before asking the customer to do anything.

## Damaged or Defective Items
- If an item arrives damaged or defective, we replace it free of charge — replacement, not repair.
- The customer should report the damage within 48 hours of delivery with a photo of the item and the packaging. Support may accept later reports with judgement; the 48-hour line exists to keep courier claims workable, not to trap customers.
- If a replacement is out of stock, the customer chooses between waiting for restock or a full refund.
- Hand-finished products (like the Glow Diya Set) have natural variation; variation is not a defect, and support should explain that warmly, with the "no two alike" framing from the spec sheet.

## What Cannot Be Returned
- Used candles (lit wicks) and washed textiles, for hygiene and resale reasons.
- Products bought in a final-sale clearance, which are always labelled as such before purchase.
- Gift notes are printed per order and have no return value; they are simply not charged for in the first place.

## Refunds
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–7 business days of the returned item passing check-in at the warehouse.
- Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded to a bank account or UPI ID the customer provides.
- Shipping fees (the flat ₹79, when charged) are refunded only when the return is our fault — damage, defect, or wrong item shipped.

## Support Channels and Hours
- Email and chat support, Monday to Saturday, 9:00 to 19:00 IST.
- First-response target: within one business day. During festive flights (Diwali especially), support staffs up to hold that target.
- Support speaks in the brand voice: friendly neighbour, plain words, no scripts that sound like scripts. "We'll sort this out" beats "your request has been escalated".

## Approved Copy Lines About Returns
- "Easy 10-day returns, free pickup included."
- "Arrived damaged? Send us a photo within 48 hours and a replacement is on us."
- Never "no-questions-asked returns" — we do ask one question (what went wrong), because it feeds product fixes.
- Never "100% satisfaction guaranteed" — "guaranteed" is a banned word, and the promise is unverifiable anyway.

## Escalation
- Any return dispute that touches a legal threat, a platform complaint, or a regulator goes straight to the compliance escalation path: pause, notify the campaign lead and legal, preserve records.
