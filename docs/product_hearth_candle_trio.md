## Specifications
- Wax: soy-blend wax with cotton wicks. No lead-core wicks — verified in the materials and safety notes.
- Jar: amber glass, reusable once the wax is finished; label peels clean.
- Burn time: approximately 18 hours per candle under normal conditions.
- Scents: Morning Chai (spiced, warm), Rain on Mango Leaves (green, fresh), Warm Sandalwood (woody, soft).
- Packaging: trio gift box with a printed scent card.

## Usage and Care
- Trim the wick to about 5 mm before each burn; keep the first burn long enough to melt the surface evenly.
- Burn on a heat-safe surface, away from drafts, curtains, children, and pets.
- Stop using when about 1 cm of wax remains at the base.

## What Copy Can and Cannot Say
This product sits closest to the compliance line, so copy discipline matters most here.
- CAN: describe the scent and the mood of the room. "Fills the room with a warm chai scent" is the approved pattern.
- CANNOT: any wellness claim. The candles do not "reduce stress", "help you sleep", "purify air", or "calm anxiety". This is the standing example in our messaging one-pager, and it is absolute.
- CANNOT: "therapeutic", "aromatherapy", or anything that implies a health outcome.
- CANNOT: "natural soy candle" as a purity claim — the wax is a soy blend; say "soy-blend wax" exactly.
- CAN: "reusable amber jar" — reuse is a plain product fact, not an environmental claim, but do not extend it to "zero waste" or "sustainable".
