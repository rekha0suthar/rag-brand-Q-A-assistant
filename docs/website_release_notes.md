# Website Release Notes

Customer-visible changes to the Taara Home storefront, newest first. Marketing uses these to know what the site can actually do before promising it in copy; support uses them to answer "since when" questions.

## v2.6 — 12 September 2025
- Festive landing page template added for campaign flights; the Diwali 2025 page is its first use. Campaign leads can request a landing page at asset-lock time.
- Bundle builder (beta): shoppers can combine any two Everyday-band or Signature-band items into a single gift box. Bundles price as the sum of items — the builder never discounts, keeping it clean of offer rules.
- Fixed: care instructions tab occasionally failed to render on slow connections.

## v2.5 — 28 July 2025
- Pincode-based delivery estimates on every product page. The estimate shows a date range, not a promise; copy and support must call it an estimate.
- Reverse-pickup eligibility by pincode now shows at checkout, matching the returns policy (free reverse pickup where available, alternatives arranged by support where not).
- Order tracking page redesigned to show plain-language statuses ("Packed", "On the way", "Out for delivery") in the brand voice.

## v2.4 — 16 June 2025
- "Your Corners" UGC gallery launched: a browsable wall of customer photos, each used with logged permission and creator credit, consistent with the UGC rules. Shoppers can submit photos directly from the order page.
- Product pages can now embed a gallery strip of UGC photos for that product.
- Summer 2025 campaign assets integrated on the homepage during the flight window.

## v2.3 — 5 May 2025
- Handwritten-style gift note at checkout: shoppers type a message and choose one of three note designs; it prints in a handwriting-style face and ships inside the gift box. Included at no charge on every order, like gift wrap.
- Gift note has a 240-character limit and a profanity filter; support can reprint a garbled note as a goodwill gesture under the returns policy's spirit, though notes themselves are not returnable items.
- Checkout now states the free-shipping threshold (₹1,499) with a plain progress line ("₹300 away from free shipping") — informational, not a countdown, so it carries no offer disclaimer.

## v2.2 — 20 March 2025
- Collection pages reorganised around the three price bands (Everyday, Signature, Celebration) so the catalogue reads the way the pricing policy describes it.
- Search now understands common Hinglish queries ("diya", "ghar decor").
- Accessibility pass: focus states, alt text requirements for all product imagery, larger tap targets on mobile.

## v2.1 — 2 February 2025
- Email unsubscribe moved to one click, and the physical mailing address block standardised in every email footer, matching the compliance rules for email campaigns.
- Wishlist launched; wishlist reminders are plain notifications and never carry manufactured urgency.

## Notes for Copywriters
- Only promise features listed here. "Gift note at checkout" is promisable since v2.3; "bundle builder" must be called a beta until these notes say otherwise.
- Feature names in copy match the names here exactly — "Your Corners", not "our community gallery".
