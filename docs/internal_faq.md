# Internal FAQ: Marketing and Content Operations

Working answers to the operational questions the team asks most. Policies live in their own documents (brand guidelines, compliance, pricing, returns); this FAQ is the "how do I actually do it" layer.

## When does my asset need compliance review?
Whenever it contains an offer, a price, a partnership label, or a claim about the product. Tone-only content (a mood post with no price or claim) needs the writer's self-check and the campaign lead's review, not the legal queue. When unsure, send it — a two-day wait beats a pulled asset.

## How long does compliance review take?
Two business days, counted from when the asset lands in the legal queue complete — copy, visual, and placement together. Partial submissions restart the clock. During festive season the queue fills early; book review slots the same week assets lock, not launch week.

## My asset was approved three months ago. Can I reuse it?
Check the approval date: approvals expire after 90 days. Past that, resubmit before scheduling — this applies even to unchanged assets, because the rules themselves may have changed. Diwali 2024 assets, for example, all need fresh review before any 2025 reuse.

## Can I quote a price in a social caption?
Yes, plainly: "₹499" is fine. The moment the caption frames it as an offer ("now ₹499", "₹499 this week only"), it becomes offer copy — disclaimer plus compliance review required.

## Where do I find approved copy lines?
Reference lines live on each product's spec sheet, and campaign briefs carry pre-approved copy starters. The before/after examples document shows the voice in action. If a line is not marked approved somewhere, treat it as a draft.

## Who settles a copy disagreement?
The campaign lead, with the brand guidelines open. If the disagreement touches a claim or an offer, it skips the debate and goes to compliance review (this mirrors the tone FAQ — it is the same rule).

## Can I mention a competitor to say we're better value?
No. No competitor comparisons, by name or by implication — "unlike other brands" counts as implication.

## A customer DM'd asking about a return. Do I answer?
Hand it to support with a warm one-liner ("Our support folks will sort this — forwarding you now"). Marketing accounts never promise return outcomes; support applies the returns policy, which marketing copy must mirror but not improvise on.

## How do I run a giveaway?
Giveaways need full terms linked, "No purchase necessary" stated, and compliance review before announcement. Budget time accordingly; a giveaway is offer-shaped even when nothing is sold.

## What's the rule on UGC reposts?
Permission first, in writing (a DM reply counting as written yes), logged before the repost goes live. Credit the creator in the caption. UGC earned the cheapest clicks of Summer 2025, so the process is worth the small friction.

## Which discounts can I propose for a flight?
One discount, one threshold, from the two approved shapes (flat-above-threshold or quantity offer), capped at 20% — see the pricing and discount policy. Anything outside that needs a written exception before compliance sees it.

## Who updates this FAQ?
The campaign lead, after each campaign retro. If an answer here ever contradicts a policy document, the policy document wins — flag the contradiction rather than working around it.
