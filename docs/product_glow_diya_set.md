## Usage and Care
- For oil or wax tealights. Place on a stable, heat-safe surface away from curtains and fabric.
- Wipe clean with a dry cloth. Do not soak in water; terracotta is porous and the paint can lift.
- Store in the box between festivals to avoid chipping.
