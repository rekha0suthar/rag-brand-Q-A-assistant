# Summer 2025 Campaign Retro

## Overview
- Theme: "Sunny side of home" — light refresh of living spaces for the summer break.
- Flight: 15 May to 30 June 2025, across Instagram, YouTube Shorts, and email.
- Offer structure: Flat 15% off on orders above ₹1,499. Disclaimer "Limited-time offer; terms apply." ran on every asset.

## What Worked
- Keywords that resonated: "breezy", "refresh", "slow mornings", "summer corner".
- Carousel posts showing a single room styled three ways outperformed single-image posts by roughly 2x on saves.
- Short headlines (under six words) with one emoji kept the playful tone without feeling noisy.
- Email subject line "Your balcony called. It wants cushions." had the best open rate of the flight.
- User-generated content reposts (with permission and credit) drove the cheapest clicks of the campaign.

## What Didn't Work
- Long-form YouTube ads (30s+) had high drop-off; we cut them mid-flight.
- The phrase "deal of the season" tested poorly — it reads as a superlative and was flagged in review. Avoid it.
- Running the offer banner on every story frame fatigued viewers; one offer frame per story set is enough.

## Copy Examples
- "Make room for slow mornings."
- "Breezy fits for your living room."
- "Small corner, big calm."

## Learnings for Next Flight
- Keep offers simple: one discount, one threshold. The Buy 2 Get 10% Off structure from Diwali 2024 confused some summer shoppers when combined with the flat discount; never run two offer structures in the same flight.
- Book compliance review at least a week before launch; two assets missed the first drop because the review queue was full.
- Carousels and UGC first, polished studio shots second.
- Emoji budget: one per caption, none in email subject lines after the first.

## Numbers (directional)
- Saves per post: carousels ~2x singles.
- Email open rate: best subject line cleared the campaign average by a wide margin.
- Cheapest CPC: UGC reposts.
- Mid-flight cut: long-form video, reallocated to Shorts.
