# Materials, Ingredients and Safety Notes

The verified facts behind every material claim in our copy. If a claim is not in this document, copy may not make it — "I'm pretty sure it's cotton" is not verification. Compliance treats unverified material claims exactly like unverified environmental claims.

## Verified Material Facts

### Terracotta products (Glow Diya Set and other clay decor)
- Natural terracotta clay, kiln-fired.
- Painted with lead-free, water-based paints. "Lead-free paint" is a verified, approved claim.
- Terracotta is porous: products are decorative and not food-safe. Copy must never suggest serving or food use.
- Small variations in size, shade, and brushwork are inherent to hand-finishing — approved framing is "no two alike".

### Candles (Hearth Candle Trio and seasonal singles)
- Soy-blend wax. The approved phrase is exactly "soy-blend wax" — never "pure soy", "natural wax", or "100% soy".
- Cotton wicks, no lead-core wicks. "No lead-core wicks" is a verified, approved claim.
- Fragrances are standard cosmetic-grade fragrance oils. We make no hypoallergenic claim and no "essential oils" claim.
- Amber jars are standard soda-lime glass, reusable after cleaning. Reuse is a product fact; it is not an environmental certification and copy must not inflate it into one.

### Textiles (Breeze Cushion Cover Set and other fabric decor)
- 100% cotton, 180 GSM, conventionally grown. NOT organic — "organic cotton" is a banned claim for current SKUs.
- Dyes are standard reactive dyes, colourfast under the care instructions on the spec sheet.
- No flame-retardant treatment. Copy must never imply fire safety for any textile.

## Safety Rules for Copy and Packaging
- Open-flame products (diyas, candles) always carry the full safety text on packaging: keep away from children, pets, drafts, and flammable material; never leave a flame unattended; burn on a heat-safe surface.
- Social and product-page copy for flame products must never minimise fire risk, even playfully. "Set the mood, not the curtains" was rejected in review — the joke undermines the warning.
- No product is marketed to or for children. Decor photography avoids children interacting with flame products.
- Choking-hazard text appears on any product with parts smaller than 4 cm.

## Environmental Claims: The Standing Rule
We currently hold NO approved environmental certification for any product. Therefore:
- "Eco-friendly", "sustainable", "green", "earth-friendly", "zero waste" — all banned in copy, product pages, and packaging.
- Plain facts are allowed when verified and stated without an environmental halo: "recycled-cardboard gift box" and "reusable amber jar" are fine; "sustainable packaging" is not.
- If a certification is ever obtained, legal adds it to this document first; copy follows this document, never the other way around.

## Verification Process
- New material claims are verified against supplier documentation by the sourcing team, then added here.
- This document is reviewed each quarter, aligned with the seasonal collection refresh (four times a year).
- When a spec sheet and this document disagree, this document wins, and the spec sheet gets corrected.
