# Product Messaging One-Pager: Home Decor & Gifting Range

## Positioning
We make everyday home decor and gifting that feels personal without being pricey. The one-line positioning: "Little joys for every home." Lead with feeling (warmth, calm, celebration), support with product detail, close with ease of buying.

## Message House

### Roof (the one thing)
Small, thoughtful pieces that make a house feel like home.

### Pillar 1: Thoughtful design
- Talk about the small details: hand-finished edges, festive motifs, colours picked for Indian homes and light.
- Proof points: design team iterates with customer photo feedback; seasonal collections refresh four times a year.

### Pillar 2: Easy gifting
- Every product ships gift-ready: wrapped, with a handwritten-style note option at checkout.
- Proof points: gifting was a top keyword in Diwali 2024; "Light up your gifting game ✨" remains our reference copy line.

### Pillar 3: Easy on the pocket
- Say "easy on the pocket", never "cheap" and never "luxury". We sit in the accessible middle.
- Proof points: most products under ₹999; free shipping threshold at ₹1,499.

## What We Never Say
- No superlatives ("best", "No.1", "guaranteed") — banned brand-wide.
- No health or wellness benefits. A diffuser "fills the room with a warm scent"; it does not "reduce stress".
- No environmental claims without an approved certification.
- No competitor comparisons.

## Boilerplate (approved)
Short: "Little joys for every home."
Medium: "We make home decor and gifts that feel personal — thoughtful design, gift-ready packaging, and prices that stay easy on the pocket."

## Channel Notes
- Product pages: detail first (size, material, care), feeling second. Plain English.
- Social: feeling first, one emoji max, playful headlines under six words.
- Email: benefit-led subject lines, no emoji in subjects, disclaimer on any offer.

## Required Elements on Offer Messaging
Any message with a price or discount must carry "Limited-time offer; terms apply." and clear the compliance review before it is scheduled.
