# Monsoon 2025 Campaign Brief

## Overview
- Theme: "Cozy Corner Season" — the rains keep everyone home; help them love being there.
- Flight: 10 July to 20 August 2025.
- Channels: Instagram (carousels + stories), YouTube Shorts, email. No long-form video — the Summer 2025 retro cut it for high drop-off and we are not re-testing it this flight.
- Hero product: Hearth Candle Trio, supported by the Breeze Cushion Cover Set.

## Offer
- Free shipping on ALL orders for the flight window (the one exception window to the ₹1,499 threshold, approved as this flight's single offer).
- Per the pricing policy: this is the flight's one offer. No percentage discount runs alongside it.
- Disclaimer "Limited-time offer; terms apply." on every asset that mentions the offer, as always.
- Offer end date is real and fixed: 20 August 2025, stated plainly in the terms.

## Audience and Insight
Monsoon traffic skews to evening browsing on phones. People are not redecorating in the rains — they are nesting. The insight: a monsoon purchase is a mood purchase, usually one or two small items, not a cart full. That is why the offer removes the shipping barrier on small orders instead of discounting.

## Creative Direction
- Keywords to build on: "cozy", "rainy evening", "warm corner", "chai weather", "slow".
- Palette: amber, deep green, grey skies through a window. Lamp-lit rooms, not studio lighting.
- Formats: carousels first ("one corner, three rainy moods" — reusing the three-ways format that won Summer 2025), UGC reposts second, polished shots last.
- Emoji budget: one per caption, none in email subjects — standard rules apply.

## Copy Starters (pre-approved directions, still need final review)
- "Chai weather. Candle weather."
- "Let it pour. You're home."
- "A warm corner for a grey day."
- Hinglish is welcome where natural: "Baarish + diya = sorted."

## What To Avoid
- No wellness angles on the candles. "Fills the room with a warm chai scent" is the ceiling; "melts the monsoon blues away" is a mood-outcome claim and will be flagged.
- No "monsoon sale" framing — this flight is an offer on shipping, not a sale, and "sale" invites superlative creep.
- No weather-damage fear angles ("protect your home from damp") — we sell joy, not anxiety.

## Timeline and Process
- Asset lock: 27 June. Compliance review takes two business days and the queue fills before flights — book it the same week, not launch week. Summer 2025 lost two assets to a full queue; do not repeat that.
- UGC permissions: collected and logged before repost, with credit in the caption.
- Mid-flight check on 25 July: if story-frame fatigue shows up, drop to one offer frame per story set immediately (standing learning from Summer 2025).

## Success Measures (directional)
- Saves per carousel against the Summer 2025 benchmark.
- Share of orders below ₹1,499 during the flight window — the offer exists to lift exactly this.
- UGC repost volume with permissions on file.
