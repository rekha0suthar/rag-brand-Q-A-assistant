# Compliance Rules for Marketing Copy

All outbound marketing copy must pass these rules before publishing. When in doubt, route the asset through the review process below.

## Mandatory Disclosures
- Every discount or promotional offer must carry the disclaimer: "Limited-time offer; terms apply."
- Paid partnerships and influencer posts must be labelled "#ad" or "Paid partnership" at the top of the caption, not buried in hashtags.
- Price comparisons ("was/now" pricing) must reference a price actually charged in the last 30 days.
- Sweepstakes and giveaways must link to full terms and state "No purchase necessary."
- Email campaigns must include the physical mailing address and a working unsubscribe link in the footer.

## Prohibited Claims
- No superlatives presented as fact: "best", "No.1", "guaranteed" are banned brand-wide (see brand guidelines).
- No health, wellness, or safety claims of any kind. We sell joy, not outcomes.
- No environmental claims ("eco-friendly", "sustainable", "green") without a certification reference approved by legal.
- No claims about competitor products, by name or by implication.
- No "free" claims when a purchase is required; say "included with purchase" instead.
- Never imply urgency that isn't real: no fake countdown timers, no "only 2 left" unless inventory data confirms it.

## Review Process
1. Writer self-checks copy against this document and the brand guidelines.
2. Campaign lead reviews for tone and offer accuracy.
3. Any asset containing an offer, a price, a partnership label, or a claim about the product goes to the compliance reviewer (legal queue) before scheduling.
4. Compliance turnaround is two business days; plan campaign timelines accordingly.
5. Approved assets are tagged with the review date. Approvals expire after 90 days; re-submit if the asset is reused later.

## Escalation
- If a live asset is flagged (by a customer, platform, or regulator), pause the placement first, then notify the campaign lead and legal.
- Keep a copy of the flagged asset and the approval record; do not edit the asset before legal has seen it.

## Quick Checklist
- Disclaimer present on every offer.
- No banned words, no health claims, no unverified green claims.
- Partnership posts labelled.
- Compliance sign-off logged for anything with a price or a claim.
