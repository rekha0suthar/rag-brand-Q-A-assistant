# Pricing and Discount Policy

How Taara Home prices products and runs offers. Campaign leads own offer design; compliance owns the disclaimer and review rules (see the compliance document). This policy keeps the two aligned.

## Price Bands
Every product sits in exactly one band. Bands keep the catalogue legible and keep us honest about the "easy on the pocket" pillar.

- Everyday band: ₹199–₹499. Impulse-friendly gifts and small decor. Example: Glow Diya Set at ₹499.
- Signature band: ₹500–₹999. The heart of the catalogue; most products live here or below. Examples: Hearth Candle Trio at ₹649, Breeze Cushion Cover Set at ₹799.
- Celebration band: ₹1,000–₹1,999. Bundles, festive hampers, and larger decor pieces. This is our ceiling; we do not list products above ₹1,999.

Repricing a product into a different band needs the campaign lead's sign-off, because band moves change which offers it can join.

## Shipping
- Free shipping on orders of ₹1,499 and above. This threshold is brand-wide and does not change per campaign.
- Orders below the threshold ship at a flat ₹79.
- Gift wrap and the handwritten-style gift note are included at no charge on every order — they are part of the product promise, never a paid add-on and never framed as a "free gift" offer.

## Offer Rules
1. One discount, one threshold, per flight. The Summer 2025 retro made this a hard rule after the Diwali 2024 Buy 2 Get 10% Off structure confused shoppers when it overlapped with a flat discount. Never run two offer structures in the same flight.
2. Discounts come in two approved shapes only: a flat percentage off above an order value (e.g. "Flat 15% off above ₹1,499"), or a quantity offer (e.g. "Buy 2, get 10% off"). Pick one.
3. The discount ceiling is 20%. Anything deeper needs a written exception from the campaign lead before it reaches compliance review.
4. Every offer carries the mandatory disclaimer "Limited-time offer; terms apply." and goes through compliance review before scheduling.
5. Offers always have a real end date set before launch. No evergreen "limited-time" offers — if it does not actually end, we may not call it limited.
6. "Was/now" strikethrough pricing must reference a price actually charged in the last 30 days, per the compliance rules.

## What We Never Do
- No "free" claims when a purchase is required; say "included with purchase".
- No stacking: a product is never in two offers at once.
- No surge or scarcity pricing, and no countdown timers we cannot back with a real end date.
- No coupon codes in influencer captions without the partnership label and compliance sign-off.

## Who Approves What
- Band assignment and reprices: campaign lead.
- Offer design within these rules: campaign lead.
- Exceptions (deeper than 20%, new offer shapes): campaign lead proposes in writing, compliance reviews.
- The disclaimer and review process itself: compliance owns it; this policy never overrides it.
